Why it’s different

Most solo travel advice is either chaotic (“just wing it”) or unrealistic (“travel forever”). This guide is built for adults with responsibilities—people who want independence, depth, and a trip that doesn’t feel like a stress test.

It’s honest, practical, and designed to be used—before your trip and during it.
