Accommodation

Start with reputable platforms, then read the details carefully. For hostels, I look for strong recent reviews, a central location, and a good solo-traveler vibe.

🏆

Hostelworld HOSCAR Awards

When I'm researching a new destination, this is often my first stop. It's one of the easiest ways to find hostels that consistently earn great reviews.

Visit Hostelworld HOSCARS
🛏️

Hostelworld

When I'm researching a new destination, this is often my first stop. The annual HOSCAR Awards highlight hostels that consistently earn excellent traveler reviews and can be a great shortcut when you're overwhelmed by choices.

What I look for:

  • 8.5+ overall rating
  • Strong Solo Traveler reviews
  • 4–6 bed dorms when available
  • Central location
  • Positive recent reviews

How I Use These Resources

I treat tools as starting points, not final answers. I compare a few options, check recent reviews, verify the location, and keep backup plans simple. The goal is not to over-plan every minute. The goal is to arrive prepared enough to relax.
