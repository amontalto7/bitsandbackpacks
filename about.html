About Bits & Backpacks

Solo travel advice for adults who want freedom without chaos.

Why this exists

I didn’t start traveling solo to prove anything. I started because I was curious—and because I didn’t want to wait for the perfect timing, the perfect travel partner, or the perfect plan. What I found wasn’t just new cities. It was clarity. Independence. Confidence built through small decisions made alone.

A different approach

A lot of solo travel content is either chaotic (“just wing it”) or unrealistic (“travel forever”). This site is built for adults—people with careers, responsibilities, and limited time—who still want meaningful travel and the quiet satisfaction of doing it their own way.

What you’ll find here

Mindset

Fear, confidence, loneliness, social energy—plus the mental shifts that make solo travel feel empowering instead of intimidating.

Logistics

Planning that’s useful (not obsessive): destinations, pacing, neighborhoods, transit, and how to make travel smoother with fewer moving parts.

Safety + Money

Practical street smarts, common scams, and money decisions that save you stress (and usually cash) on the road.

Who this is for

If you’re curious about solo travel but still hesitating, you’re the target audience. If you’ve traveled solo before and want to do it smarter, you’re also the target audience.

This is especially for people who like structure, value safety, and don’t want their vacation to feel like a stress test.

Quick note on tone

I’m not here to sell you a fantasy. I’m here to help you travel well: with clarity, confidence, and enough planning to feel grounded—without turning the trip into homework.

If that’s what you’re looking for, welcome.
